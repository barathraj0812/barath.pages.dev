Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026  ·  Effective: 19 October 2026  ·  Site: barath.pages.dev

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how barath.pages.dev (this portfolio website, operated by Barath M from Chennai, India) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you visit. This is a personal portfolio site — no accounts, no purchases, no user-generated content.

Short version: Essential storage keeps the site working. Optional analytics run only if you click Accept. Nothing is sold and there are no ads. You can say no, or change your mind, at any time.

Information Collected

Personal data is collected in only three ways:

  • Essential data — technical information needed to load the site (e.g. your IP address and browser type, via server logs managed by Cloudflare Pages), plus your cookie choice and theme preference, which stay in your own browser.
  • Analytics data — collected only if you accept optional cookies: a random visitor ID and session ID, pages visited, time on page, clicks and other interactions, referrer, device and browser type, approximate location (country and region), and IP address. This data is tied to a random ID rather than your name, but it can be linked to your browser, so it is treated as personal data.
  • Contact form — your name, email address, and message. Used solely to respond to your inquiry.

No passwords, payment details, or precise location are collected. There is no advertising and no tracking across other websites.

The GitHub Activity section does not add to this collection, but it does make your browser contact GitHub and a third-party data provider. What they may receive is explained in that section.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. This site uses two categories of cookies and similar browser storage:

Category Purpose Examples Status
Essential Required for the site to function. Cannot be disabled. Cookie consent choice, theme preference Required
Analytics Helps understand how visitors use the site so it can be improved. Set only after you accept. Random visitor ID, session ID Optional
  • Nothing in the analytics category is created or sent before you click Accept.
  • If you decline, or ignore the banner, analytics stay off and the site works exactly the same.
  • The GitHub Activity section does not set cookies and its code does not write to localStorage or sessionStorage.
  • To change your mind: clear cookies and site data for barath.pages.dev. The consent banner will appear again and your visitor ID is removed from your browser. To also delete records already collected, make a request under Your Rights.

Third-Party Services

These providers process data on my behalf, only to run the site. Your data is never sold, and it may be processed outside India.

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site (Pages), runs the analytics and contact form back end, stores analytics records, and provides the Turnstile spam check. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google — stores contact form submissions in Google Sheets, and serves the site’s fonts, so your IP address reaches Google when a page loads, before any cookie choice. policies.google.com/privacy

External links on this site (GitHub, itch.io, live apps) lead to third-party websites. This policy does not cover those sites — please review their respective privacy policies.

GitHub Activity Section

The portfolio has a GitHub Activity section showing public information about the GitHub account barathraj0812: a contribution graph, recent public activity, and repository statistics. This information is about that account, not about visitors. It comes from two outside services. They are separate from the providers listed above and do not work on my behalf in the way described there.

  • GitHub (api.github.com) — supplies the public profile, repository and recent public event data. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • github-contributions-api.jogruber.de — supplies the daily contribution calendar. It is an independent open-source service, not run by GitHub or by me. Its source code is at github.com/grubersjoe/github-contributions-api. I have not found a privacy policy published for this service, so I cannot describe how it handles request data.

When requests are made. Your browser asks these services for data when you scroll close to the GitHub Activity section, not when the site first loads. If your browser cannot detect that, the requests are made as soon as the page loads. They are repeated if you press “Try again” after an error.

What the services may receive. The requests go directly from your browser to these services, not through this site’s own back end. Like any web request, they can expose your IP address, browser and device details (user-agent) and other request metadata to the service, which may record them under its own practices. I do not receive or control those records. The requests contain the GitHub username above and fixed parameters. This site adds no visitor identifier and does not send your analytics visitor or session ID. The requests are sent without cookies and without a referrer, but your browser still sends the standard Origin header, which shows that the request comes from barath.pages.dev.

Storage. The data is held in the page’s memory while the page is open and is gone when you close or reload it. This section does not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or other persistent storage. Your browser may still keep responses in its ordinary HTTP cache, and I do not control how those services or your browser cache them.

Analytics and consent. These requests are not analytics, and the cookie banner does not control them. They happen whether you accepted or declined analytics. Nothing in the Analytics or Cookies sections above has changed.

Availability. The contribution calendar depends on the third-party service, which reads the public GitHub profile and, according to its documentation, caches results for about an hour. It may be slow, rate-limited or unavailable, and its data may be incomplete or out of date. GitHub also limits anonymous requests from a single IP address. If data cannot be loaded, the section says so instead of showing empty values.

Your choices. This site has no setting to switch these requests off. Because the section sits between Achievements and About, scrolling down the page normally triggers them. You can use your browser’s settings or a content blocker to block these hosts or scripts, though other features of the site may also stop working, and I cannot guarantee the result. Because I do not hold what these services record, I cannot access or delete it. Please contact them under their own policies for that.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Withdraw consent and opt out of analytics at any time
  • Know what personal data is held about you and get a copy
  • Have your data corrected or deleted, including contact form messages and analytics records
  • Raise a complaint if you are unhappy with how your data is handled — it will be looked into and answered

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact form on the portfolio and start your message with “Privacy request”. You will get a reply within 30 days, free of charge. You may be asked to confirm it is you, and analytics records can only be found if your visitor ID is still in your browser.

Data Retention

  • Contact messages — kept until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • Analytics records — kept until you withdraw consent and ask for them to be deleted.
  • Server logs — held by Cloudflare under its own policy.
  • GitHub Activity requests — not stored by this site. Any records kept by GitHub or the contribution-data service follow their own policies.
  • Cookie and theme preferences — stored in your browser only.

Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please decline analytics and ask a parent or guardian before using the contact form. If you believe a child has submitted information, please get in touch and it will be deleted promptly.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. The Last updated date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. If a change materially affects how your data is used, you will be asked for consent again. Continuing to use the site is never treated as consent to analytics.
